HUMANBULB Theme Concepts

Three distinct directions for the Impact Portal.

Each concept keeps the same product idea but changes the tone, layout emphasis, and presentation style. Your current working MVP stays untouched in the existing setup and dashboard pages.

Concept 1

Clean Civic

A polished nonprofit dashboard with calm green and blue tones, a familiar sidebar, and a simple upload-to-dashboard flow.

  • Best for grant reviewers and program staff
  • Feels dependable and immediately usable
  • Strong fit for your current MVP direction
Open Clean Civic

Concept 2

Modern Data Lab

A more AI-forward split-screen experience with live preview energy, brighter blues, darker framing, and a sharper product feel.

  • Best if you want the portal to feel innovative
  • Setup and dashboard feel tightly connected
  • Good for demos to partners and funders
Open Data Lab

Concept 3

Editorial Nonprofit

A warmer, report-inspired interface that blends uploads, impact storytelling, and grant readiness into a softer, more human presentation.

  • Best for narrative-driven presentations
  • Feels like an annual report meets a dashboard
  • Strong emphasis on voices and outcomes
Open Editorial
